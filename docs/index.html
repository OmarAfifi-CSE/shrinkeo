Everything You Need (And More)

We didn't cut any corners. Shrinkeo is packed with enterprise-grade features wrapped in an elegant UI.

44 Languages & Live Search

Native localization covering 95%+ of global users with a dedicated Globe Language Selector, live search bar, flag badges, and instant live translation.

Video Editing & Quick Tools

Export directly to Animated GIF, extract MP3/AAC/WAV audio, trim lossless clips, adjust playback speed (0.5x to 4x), strip GPS privacy metadata, and auto-crop.

Custom Ratios & Rotation

Apply custom canvas aspect ratios (16:10, 2:1) with padding or rotate videos by any custom angle (45°, 30°, 90°, 180°, 270°, horizontal/vertical flips).

Target Size & CRF Modes

Specify an exact target size (e.g. 25 MB for Discord) with 2-Pass VBR rate control, or visually tune quality using the professional CRF slider.

Audio Normalization & Downmix

Boost & normalize quiet lecture or vlog audio with EBU R128 Speech or Movie Dynamic filters, and downmix surround sound to Stereo (2.0) or Mono (1.0).

Video & Audio Denoise

Clean up video grain with 3D spatial-temporal filtering and eliminate mic & background fan hiss using FFT audio noise reduction.

Next-Gen Codecs & FastStart

Compress with AV1, H.265 (HEVC), H.264, or VP9. Encodes with -movflags +faststart for instant web streaming. Harness NVIDIA, AMD, or Intel GPUs.

Batch Queue & Shell Menu

Drag and drop 100+ videos to process sequentially, or right-click any video on PC to "Compress with Shrinkeo" via clean non-admin shell integration.

Satisfying Analytics

Watch live progress, get accurate ETAs, compare original vs. compressed file sizes, and track your total Global Saved Space over time!
